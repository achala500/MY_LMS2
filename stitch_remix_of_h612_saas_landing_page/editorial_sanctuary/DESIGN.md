## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an "Editorial Academic Sanctuary" engineered specifically for Sri Lankan G.C.E. Advanced Level (A/L) candidates. The A/L journey is rigorous, stressful, and cognitively demanding. The interface counters this emotional turbulence with a warm, intellectual atmosphere inspired by Claude.ai’s serene literary feel, synthesized with Apple-level micro-tactility and precision.

The system rejects clinical dashboard aesthetics and gamified neon distractions. Instead, it frames telemetry, revision tracking, and peer accountability as thoughtful, contemplative acts. The visual language conveys quiet authority, focus, and warmth—inviting deep study cycles while treating academic metrics with dignified, editorial restraint.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in paper-like materiality and distinguished academic accents:

- **Canvas Foundation**: Unbleached soft ivory (`#F3F3F0`) acts as the base canvas, mimicking heavy cotton paper to prevent eye fatigue during lengthy late-night revision sessions.
- **Card & Container Surfaces**: Pure parchment white (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by hairline tactile framing borders (`#E4E2DC`).
- **Recessed Areas & Meter Tracks**: Warm paper neutral (`#EAE8E1`) for sunken containers, inset stat cards, progress bar grooves, and segmented tab tracks.
- **Ink & Typography**:
  - **Primary Ink**: Deep inky charcoal (`#14171A`) provides high contrast without the clinical harshness of absolute black.
  - **Secondary Ink**: Warm slate (`#535862`) for metadata, subheadings, and contextual hints.
  - **Muted Ink**: Sandstone gray (`#8A8F98`) for deactivated states, borders, and timestamp markers.
- **Accents & Telemetry Stream**:
  - **Primary Editorial Accent**: Muted terracotta red (`#9E2F29`) denotes urgent accountability actions, revision pomodoro states, active highlights, and critical past-paper deadlines.
  - **Physical Science / Combined Maths**: Deep oceanic cyan (`#007788`) reserved strictly for physical science modules, physics paper drills, and mathematical proofs.
  - **Biological Science**: Balanced forest green (`#2A7B4C`) applied to biological streams, botany/zoology trackers, and ecological taxonomy studies.

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, harnessing its geometric clarity, open apertures, and subtle humanist nuances.

- **Headings & Display**: Bold and semi-bold weights feature tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`), generating an authoritative, editorial presence reminiscent of contemporary academic journals.
- **Reading & Longform Telemetry**: Body text maintains a comfortable `1.6` line-height ratio, guaranteeing uninhibited legibility during deep review of complex syllabus rubrics, marking schemes, and revision reflections.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Form labels, telemetry badges, and data indicators use slightly tracked medium-to-semibold weights to ensure rapid scanning under exam simulation timers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system with generous macro-whitespace, reflecting an uncluttered study desk:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max content boundary of 1280px. Margins rest at 40px (`2.5rem`) to maintain an immersive, book-like border.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Secondary metrics collapse into swipeable cards or stacked modular blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral edge margins. Full-width cards preserve structural hairline integrity without visual noise.

Spacing scales consistently through an 8-point typographic and spatial scale, prioritizing intentional, breathing room between analytical data points.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical paper layering, recessed cutaways, and delicate warm-toned ambient diffusion rather than stark digital drop-shadows:

- **Surface Framing (Hairline Precision)**: Every primary surface is anchored by a sharp 1px structural hairline border (`#E4E2DC`). This creates a crisp physical demarcation between layered sheets.
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F3F3F0` sits continuously behind all components.
- **Layer 1 (Recessed Insets)**: `#EAE8E1` creates sunken cavities for past-paper progress tracks, time-spent wells, and secondary telemetry data. Features an optional inner border (`rgba(20, 23, 26, 0.04)`).
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Study Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` sheets floating slightly above the canvas. Depth is supported by a dual-stage warm ambient shadow:
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(20, 23, 26, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(20, 23, 26, 0.04)`
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Timers)**: Modals, active exam countdown cards, and interactive flyouts use an amplified warm diffusion:
  - `0 8px 24px rgba(20, 23, 26, 0.06), 0 20px 48px rgba(83, 88, 98, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The design system implements a deliberate duality in geometry to express both architectural organization and organic tactility:

- **Cards, Sheets & Layout Containers**: Fixed at `16px` (`1rem`) border radius, striking a balance between tactile physical stationery and structured software containers.
- **Pills, Badges & Action Buttons**: Strictly fully rounded (`9999px`), imparting a soft, approachable, Apple-like tactile quality that balances the crisp rectangular discipline of cards.
- **Interactive Insets & Checkpoints**: Micro elements (such as checkbox containers and module progress pips) share soft radii (`4px` to `6px`) to retain legibility at small dimensions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Background `#9E2F29`, text `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded `9999px` pill radius. Padding: `12px 24px`. Tactile press states drop brightness subtly (`#872620`) with a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary Surface Button**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E2DC`, text `#14171A`. Subtle hover shift to `#F3F3F0`.
- **Subject-Specific Action Buttons**: Dedicated variant buttons matching track streams (`#007788` for Maths/Physics, `#2A7B4C` for Biology) reserved for module launches.

### Badges & Subject Tags
- Pill geometry (`9999px`) with generous internal padding (`4px 12px`).
- **Terracotta Alert**: `#9E2F29` at 10% fill with `#9E2F29` solid typography.
- **Combined Maths / Physics**: Deep cyan background tint (`rgba(0, 119, 136, 0.10)`) with crisp `#007788` text.
- **Biology Stream**: Forest green background tint (`rgba(42, 123, 76, 0.10)`) with `#2A7B4C` text.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Rendered on crisp `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with `16px` border radius, bound by `1px solid #E4E2DC`, and elevated by gentle warm ambient diffusion.
- Internal card padding scales across `20px` to `28px` to maintain generous breathing room.
- Sub-cards or secondary metric zones within a card sit recessed inside `#EAE8E1` tracks with `10px` or `12px` radii.

### Input Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF` enveloped by a tactile border `1px solid #E4E2DC`.
- Focus states transition the border to `#9E2F29` with a subtle outer glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(158, 47, 41, 0.12)`.
- Input text is `#14171A` at `15px` with placeholder set to warm slate `#535862`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkboxes use a `5px` corner radius; radios use pure circles.
- Inactive state: `1.5px solid #E4E2DC` on `#FFFFFF`.
- Selected state: Solid fill of `#9E2F29` or appropriate subject color (`#007788` / `#2A7B4C`) displaying a high-contrast white checkmark icon.

### Study Telemetry Bars & Dials
- Base groove uses the warm recessed paper hue `#EAE8E1` with `9999px` pill endpoints.
- Active fill meters utilize vibrant subject tokens (`#007788` for pure science syllabi completion, `#2A7B4C` for bio-units, and `#9E2F29` for daily accountability streaks).
- Micro numeric metrics accompany tracks in `Plus Jakarta Sans Label-Md` at `#535862`.
